# Rainbow Reboot

Bring the colors back to a lifeless orchard.

## Story

A curse has taken the color from a magical orchard. Its five trees need your help.

You are a unicorn. Your horn can shoot a rainbow beam. Use this magic to clear the cursed fruit and bring life back to the trees.

Look around and act before the fruit rots. Each hit helps a tree recover. Miss too many fruits, and the tree loses some of its progress. Once a tree is fully healed, it stays safe.

Restore all five trees to save the orchard.

## Controls

Rainbow Reboot requires a VR headset and a browser with WebXR support.

- Open the game in your headset browser and enter VR.
- Look at a fruit to aim. Turn your head to find fruit around you.
- Press the controller trigger to shoot a rainbow beam from your horn.
- Aim with your head, not with your controller.

You do not need to walk. Stay in one place and look around the orchard.
